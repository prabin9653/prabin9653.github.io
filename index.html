Common questions

Frequently asked questions

What does a digital marketing expert do?
▾
A digital marketing expert plans and runs a business's online growth end-to-end — SEO, paid ads, social media, email marketing, content, and analytics — aligning every channel with measurable revenue and lead-generation goals. Prabin Neupane works across all of these as a Digital Marketing Specialist & SEO Lead for SMEs in the US and Australia.
How do I evaluate a digital marketing expert's portfolio before hiring?
▾
Look for verifiable case studies with real metrics (traffic, ROI, indexing, conversions), a clear breakdown of the strategies used, current certifications, and direct client testimonials. Prabin's case studies section documents exact percentage gains, timelines, and the technical work performed for each client.
What should I ask a potential digital marketing specialist?
▾
Ask about their reporting cadence, which tools they use (GA4, Search Console, Google Ads), past results with similar-sized businesses, and how they measure ROI. Prabin reports through GA4 and Search Console dashboards and has delivered 70+ SEO projects and 5 Google Ads campaigns for SME clients.
How much does it cost to hire a digital marketing expert?
▾
Pricing depends on scope — a single SEO audit costs less than an ongoing multi-channel retainer covering SEO, Google Ads, and social media. Prabin offers flexible packages for SMEs; reach out via the contact form for a free consultation and custom quote.
Is Prabin Neupane available for freelance or remote digital marketing work?
▾
Yes. Prabin is based in Kathmandu, Nepal, and is available for freelance projects and remote roles, currently serving clients in the US and Australia in addition to his role at Jasper IT Solutions.
