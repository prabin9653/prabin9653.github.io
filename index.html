Jasper IT's first B2B email campaign: an automated five-email Brevo sequence for each of four Australian local-service industries, built to generate qualified Local SEO audit enquiries and win new digital marketing clients. Each sequence covered Google visibility, industry-specific local search opportunities, competitor visibility, free SEO audit deliverables, and a final follow-up. All four sequences ran to 100% completion.

Industries targeted
PlumbingRepairs, maintenance and installation contractors
CleaningResidential and commercial cleaning businesses
RoofingRoof restoration, repair and roofing contractors
PaintingResidential, exterior and commercial painters
Key findings
  • Roofing Email 3 (competitor positioning) had the highest CTR at 6.59% and 18 unique clicks; Roofing Email 5 reached a 43.59% click-to-open rate.
  • Plumbing produced 83 unique clickers at an 8.22% CTR, with 59 soft bounces and 15 unsubscribes — pointing to list-hygiene work.
  • The campaign generated 2 trackable leads (Roofing and Painting), both received audits, and neither converted to a customer — clicks and opens alone were not business outcomes.
Reported honestly: engagement did not convert into customers. Faster follow-up, dedicated landing pages per industry, and stronger Brevo + GA4 + GTM attribution are the focus of Campaign V3.
